HOW TO PLAY

Before You Breathe

The balloon fills on every exhale — but only if each exhale lasts at least twice its inhale.

1. Breathe IN — paced, 4s. Balloon holds.
2. Hold — 1s.
3. Breathe OUT — gently, until the ratio hits 1:2.0 (8s). Hold Space or press and hold the balloon.

Qualified exhale → the balloon inflates and holds air.
Stop early → it leaks (−10 fill). Blow too hard (press X) → it bursts (−18).
3 qualified breaths reach 100%. The target is 80 to release.
Why it works: Paced slow breathing (~4.5–6.5 bpm) maximizes HRV and helps reduce acute stress. IN 4s + HOLD 1s + OUT 8s ≈ 4.6 breaths/min.
